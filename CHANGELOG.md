# Version 12: map consistency and Israeli withdrawal

## Regional map shading

The later chapter maps now use the same land and sea colours sampled from the Chapter 1 geographic map. The earlier regional image was too dark on land.

## Hezbollah locations

Labels in the 1993, 2006 and 2024 scenes have been simplified and reorganised. Geographic points are not presented as verified military bases. Source areas and destination communities are distinguished where appropriate.

## Withdrawal from southern Lebanon in 2000

The map has three distinct states.

1. Before withdrawal: the approximate southern Lebanon security zone is shaded amber and a schematic UN Blue Line is shown.
2. During withdrawal: the security zone fades and three independent cyan animated routes point towards Israel, with animated movement markers.
3. After withdrawal: the old zone remains as an outline and the map identifies geographic areas where Hezbollah remained active.

All military movement lines and the security zone are illustrative, not exact mapped positions. The United Nations used the Blue Line to verify Israeli withdrawal. It is not an agreed international border.

## Implementation and checks

The project still works as static files on GitHub Pages. The complete six chapter decision sequence and all 18 map intel states were checked in a browser. Desktop and mobile map screenshots were generated and reviewed. Core historical text, media and branding were preserved.
