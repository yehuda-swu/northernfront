# Northern Front: The Security Cabinet

## Historical sources and reference notes

The game now has SIX chapters. Its point of view is the Israeli security cabinet. Adviser dialogue, decision options and alternative consequences are fictional educational reconstructions. The recorded historical outcomes are fixed.

### Chapter 01: 1970s attacks and Operation Litani in 1978

* Jewish Film Centre, Avivim School Bus Bombing: https://jfc.org.il/en/news_journal/59606-2/105238-2/
* Historical study of attacks in the Galilee during the 1970s: https://artefacts-discovery.researcher.life/full_text/DA-2/ca/ca545cea1fdf3dd684aa4b7c94d6db4e/full_text/2bdd018140510d56ff365d1f5d1e1981.pdf
* Israel National Insurance Institute, Maalot victim record: https://laad.btl.gov.il/Web/He/TerrorVictims/Page/Default.aspx?ID=37853
* UNIFIL, background: https://unifil.unmissions.org/en/background
* Security Council Report chronology: https://www.securitycouncilreport.org/chronology/lebanon.php?print=true

Teaching notes: The three earlier attacks are 1970 (Avivim), 1974 (Kiryat Shmona) and 1974 (Maalot). They are historical context, NOT the immediate trigger of the March 1978 invasion, which followed the Coastal Road attack. Sources disagree on some injury totals and on how deaths are counted in Maalot, so the game uses conservative shared figures.

### Chapter 02: First Lebanon War, 1982

* UNIFIL: https://unifil.unmissions.org/en/background
* Security Council Report chronology: https://www.securitycouncilreport.org/chronology/lebanon.php?print=true
* Kahan Commission report, February 1983: https://www.palquest.org/en/node/9664

The June 1982 assassination attempt on Ambassador Shlomo Argov was by the Abu Nidal organisation, a rival of the PLO. The 1982 video is a 78 second edit from the longer AP watermarked footage supplied for this activity.

### Chapter 03: Hezbollah's emergence and Operation Accountability, 1993

* Congressional Research Service, origins and development: https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/20101008_R41446_74080e475b543f8ec66c3a60d2e821ecacedd810.html
* Economic Cooperation Foundation, Operation Accountability: https://www.ecf.org.il/issues/issue/1384
* Council on Foreign Relations, Hezbollah: https://www.cfr.org/backgrounders/what-hezbollah

Hezbollah emerged in the early 1980s with Iranian support, distinct from the Amal movement. Hassan Nasrallah became its leader in 1992. Operation Accountability took place in July 1993. The 68 second news clip is a modern retrospective, not an archival film from 1993.

### Chapter 04: Withdrawal from southern Lebanon, 2000

* UNIFIL background: https://unifil.unmissions.org/en/background
* UNIFIL Blue Line and operations: https://unifil.unmissions.org/en/unifil-operations

The Blue Line is a withdrawal verification line drawn by the UN. It is not the same as a bilaterally agreed international border.

### Chapter 05: Second Lebanon War, 2006

* UNIFIL background: https://unifil.unmissions.org/en/background
* UNIFIL mandate and implementation: https://unifil.unmissions.org/en/unifil-mandate
* UN Security Council Resolution 1701: https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/581053
* Security Council Report: https://www.securitycouncilreport.org/update-report/lookup_c_glkwlemtisg_b_1862907.php

Resolution 1701 called for cessation of hostilities, Israeli withdrawal, Lebanese forces deploying, disarmament commitments, restricted foreign arms transfers, a security area from the Blue Line to the Litani River, and an expanded role for UNIFIL. Adoption did not equal full compliance. The supplied illustrated Katyusha map does not record individual verified strikes. Goldwasser and Regev were abducted on 12 July 2006; their bodies were returned in 2008.

The 2024 chapter stops at the November 2024 cessation of hostilities. It does not claim the conflict was finally resolved.
