# Northern Front: The Security Cabinet

## Sources and historical references

This educational game is developed from an Israeli government perspective. The historical events are drawn from the references below. Adviser dialogue, alternative choices and their possible consequences are teaching reconstructions, not verbatim quotations, historical predictions or a score for a correct answer.

### Chapter 1, 1978

* UNIFIL, Background and history: https://unifil.unmissions.org/en/background
* Security Council Report, Lebanon chronology: https://www.securitycouncilreport.org/chronology/lebanon.php?print=true

### Chapter 2, 1982

* UNIFIL, Background and history: https://unifil.unmissions.org/en/background
* Security Council Report, Lebanon chronology: https://www.securitycouncilreport.org/chronology/lebanon.php?print=true
* Kahan Commission report, 7 February 1983, full text: https://www.palquest.org/en/node/9664

Important distinction: the shooting of Ambassador Shlomo Argov in June 1982 was by the Abu Nidal organisation, not the PLO.

### Chapter 3, 2000

* UNIFIL, Background and history: https://unifil.unmissions.org/en/background
* UNIFIL, Blue Line: https://unifil.unmissions.org/en/unifil-operations

Important distinction: the UN Blue Line verifies Israeli withdrawal and is not a mutually agreed international border.

### Chapter 4, 2006

* UNIFIL, Background and history: https://unifil.unmissions.org/en/background
* UNIFIL, mandate: https://unifil.unmissions.org/en/unifil-mandate
* Security Council Report, 2006 Lebanon war: https://www.securitycouncilreport.org/update-report/lookup_c_glkwlemtisg_b_1862907.php
* United Nations, Resolution 1701: https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/581053

### Chapter 5, 2024

* UK House of Commons Library, Israel and Hezbollah conflict 2023 to 2024: https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10108/
* Joint French and United States statement, 26 November 2024: https://www.elysee.fr/en/emmanuel-macron/2024/11/26/joint-statement-by-president-biden-and-president-macron-announcing-a-cessation-of-hostilities
* IDF, Timeline of Operation Northern Arrows, an Israeli institutional account: https://www.idf.il/en/mini-sites/idf-press-releases-israel-at-war/the-northern-front/a-timeline-of-operation-northern-arrows/
* UN Security Council Resolution 1701: https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/581053
